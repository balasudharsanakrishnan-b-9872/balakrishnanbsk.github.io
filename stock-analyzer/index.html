Honest scope. On the Vercel deployment, serverless functions proxy Yahoo Finance server-side, so this covers price/volume history → technicals, trend, returns, volatility, beta, relative strength, plus fundamentals (P/E, P/B, ROE, margins, debt, cash flow, growth) feeding a transparent score. Data is delayed, never real-time, and always timestamped. Ownership, news & backtesting need further data integrations and are shown as unavailable rather than invented. (On static hosting without the /api functions, the app falls back to public relays and fundamentals show as unavailable.)
